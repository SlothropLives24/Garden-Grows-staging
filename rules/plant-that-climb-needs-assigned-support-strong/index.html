A plant that must climb needs an assigned support strong enough to carry it.

Well established · fatal if ignored → block

How it works

Mechanical. Unsupported pole beans sprawl, rot, and yield poorly.

What to do about it

Assign a support that provides strong — a trellis or cattle panel — or choose a bush cultivar group.

The evidence

Evidence: settled without a trial - mechanics.
