Living mulch suppresses weeds and conserves soil moisture.

A good hunch A person has checked it against its sources. If you skip this, it still grows, just less well. The planner suggests it.

How it works

A living cover keeps a leaf canopy over the ground. It intercepts light at the soil surface, so weed seedlings are shaded out before they establish, and it slows surface evaporation, so the soil holds more water. In a Three Sisters planting this is the squash's role. Its broad leaves shade the bed.

The evidence

Evidence: checked against 2 sources in the research-based literature.

How we grade evidence

Well established
The rule names its mechanism and is settled, by sources a person has read and checked or by plain arithmetic and geometry.
Promising
The mechanism is named and the literature backs it. A few questions are still open.
A good hunch
Plausible by its mechanism, but field studies are thin, so treat it as a lean.
Contested
The evidence points both ways, or no study has tested it, so the planner does not enforce it.
Refuted
The claim did not survive its sources. It is kept so the folklore can be answered.
