When lettuce and spinach bolt, summer greens keep growing

As recorded: In the months a place's lettuce and spinach run to seed in the heat, Malabar spinach, New Zealand spinach, amaranth and sweet potato leaves keep producing leaf, given steady water and feeding.

A good hunch A person has checked it against its sources. If you skip this, it still grows, just less well. The planner suggests it.

The line has a run for each of its 6 sources. Each one’s date is the day a person read it.

How to read this
  • Each run of the line is one group, as long as its count.
  • It is one line, wound from the middle out, in the order of the list.

How it works

Heat is what sends lettuce and spinach to seed. These four are warm-season leaf crops that grow best in the heat that ends a lettuce crop, so the same months are their season. Dry soil or a hungry plant sends them to flower too, and then the leaves turn bitter and growth stops.

What to do about it

In the months your lettuce bolts, grow one of these for your greens instead. Keep the soil moist and feed them.

The evidence

Evidence: checked against 6 sources in the research-based literature.
