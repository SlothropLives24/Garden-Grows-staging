Sources we read

The rules and folklore on this site cite 394 sources from 50 publishers. For 299 of them, the record holds the day a person read the source, and the pages that cite one show it as “Read by a person on” that day.

Any other date shown is the day the page was fetched and quoted, marked “Accessed”

Whether a person has checked a rule against its sources is a mark of its own, beside the rule’s grade.

Of the 331 sources the rules cite, 296 are university extension guides, 12 are peer-reviewed studies and 23 are government and other publications

Extension guides are written by university specialists from the research, so they are good sources, but they are a step away from the trials themselves: 4 of the 108 source-checked rules graded Well established or Promising cite a study directly.
