Both families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org), which permits bundling with software. Files are
the latin-subset variable woff2 builds from the Google Fonts service, vendored
because the app's Content-Security-Policy allows no third-party hosts.

- `BricolageGrotesque[opsz,wdth,wght].woff2` — Bricolage Grotesque,

  (https://github.com/marcologous/hanken-grotesk).
