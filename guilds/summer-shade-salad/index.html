Summer shade salad

Lettuce planted where sweet corn shades it through the hottest part of the day, so it keeps going longer into summer.

Between seasons

What it claims

The tall crop's midsummer shadow shelters the greens from the heat that makes them bolt

Promising

How it works: Leaf temperature. Shade from a taller crop lowers the leaf and soil temperature of the greens beneath it, and high summer temperature is the trigger that sends these species to seed and turns them bitter.

Evidence: checked against 5 sources in the research-based literature.

Who does what, and the swaps

Each job in this planting can be done by more than one plant. The usual choice comes first, and each swap says what it costs.

Shade

  • Usual choice: Sweet corn
  • Swaps: Giant single-stem sunflower, Vining tomato
  • What a swap costs: A giant sunflower casts the shade, but its residue suppresses the small-seeded greens sown beside it; an indeterminate tomato needs a stake to stand that tall and is a heavy feeder. Neither needs the corn's pollination block.

Bolter

  • Usual choice: Leaf lettuce
  • Swaps: Spinach, Radicchio, Mizuna

Rules that decide where it fits

Corn needs a block of at least three short rows side by side for good kernel set, and four rows is better.

Well established

If you skip this, the planting fails. The planner won’t allow it.

How it works: Maize is wind-pollinated and protandrous (each plant sheds its pollen before its own silks are ready). Tassels shed pollen that must land on the silks of neighboring plants. In a single row much of the pollen blows out of the row, and each silk it misses is one missing kernel.

Evidence: checked against 5 sources in the research-based literature.

The 5 sources

extension.umn.edu Accessed July 11, 2026

Growing sweet corn in home gardens UMN Extension
Always plant corn in blocks of at least four rows. Corn planted in a single row will have much of its pollen blown out of the row, and will produce ears that have blank areas where kernels did not form.

content.ces.ncsu.edu Accessed July 11, 2026

Organic Sweet Corn Production NC State Extension
Corn is wind-pollinated and should be planted in blocks of at least 4 rows for good pollination to occur.

extension.umd.edu Read by a person on October 10, 2026

Growing Sweet Corn in a Home Garden UMD Extension
Plant in blocks of at least three to four short rows, rather than one or two long rows, to ensure good pollination and full kernel development.

hgic.clemson.edu Accessed July 11, 2026

Sweet Corn Clemson HGIC 1308
This crop is wind-pollinated; therefore, plant in blocks of several rows rather than 1 or 2 long rows to ensure full ears.

richmond.ces.ncsu.edu Read by a person on October 3, 2026

Glass Gem Corn NC State Extension, Richmond County Center
When tassels start dropping pollen, stalks can be gently shaken. This mimics wind and will release the pollen to float down to the silks.
Remedy

Plant corn in a block of several short rows rather than one long row; four rows is best. For a small patch, shake the stalks gently while the tassels shed pollen, or dust a cut tassel over the silks, every few days. Or leave corn out and give the beans a trellis.

For rotation, a plant team's crop families count across its whole bed

Promising

If you skip this, you lose real yield. The planner warns you.

How it works: Soilborne inoculum and overwintering pest stages disperse across the bed. A plant team dominated by one family forecloses that ground for that family's rotation interval.

Evidence: checked against 2 sources in the research-based literature.

Remedy

Rotate the whole bed the plant team occupied, including rows that family never grew in.
