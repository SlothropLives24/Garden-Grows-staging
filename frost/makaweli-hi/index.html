Frost dates for Makaweli, Hawaii

Frost is rare here. In a typical year this station records no freeze at all, so there is no last-frost date to plan around and the growing season runs all year.

Each crop’s range of monthly temperatures is an estimate from one extension table, so treat these months as a guide.

Where these numbers come from

NOAA NCEI 1991–2020 Climate Normals, the Makaweli station (listed by NOAA as Makaweli, USC00515864), at 21.92, -159.63. These are that station’s own records, unshifted. The further your garden is from it, the less exactly they describe your ground. The planner finds the nearest station to your garden and lowers its confidence with distance, so no number is presented as if it were measured in your garden.

Cold air also pools in low ground on the clear, still nights when frost happens, so a hollow can freeze while a slope a few hundred feet away does not. No table can know that about your garden. A few seasons of your own logged frosts can.
