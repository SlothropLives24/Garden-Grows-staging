Frost dates for Bucaramanga, Colombia

Frost is rare here. In 30 years of a weather model’s daily lows, not one night reached freezing, so the growing season runs all year.

Growing season
All year
Hardiness zone nearby
12b, estimated from the weather model

April, May, August and September, in hot, long days (how we know)
From a weather model, not a station; no long record nearby to check it against · A good hunch

The weather model alone, graded A good hunch. Whether frost comes here, and how often, is read from the weather model’s square centered 19 km (12 miles) away.

See where every city’s dates come from on the coverage map.

Modeled, not measured. No weather station stands behind these dates. They come from ERA5, a reconstruction of the world’s weather from 1991 to 2020 made by ECMWF, Europe’s medium-range weather forecasting agency, for the EU’s Copernicus program, and read here through Open-Meteo. They are the model’s daily lows for the point at 7.25, -73.25, 19 km (12 miles) from the center of Bucaramanga. A frost here means a low at or below 0 °C (32 °F).

A weather model sees the land in squares about 25 km (16 miles) across, so it can miss what sets one garden apart from the next: a frost hollow, a hillside, a lakeshore, a city’s own warmth. The Milpa planner reads these same numbers for a garden here and grades them A good hunch.

The weather model reads this square on ground about 685 m (2,247 ft) lower than Bucaramanga, where its temperatures run several degrees off, so this page shows the months it marks and leaves its numbers out.
