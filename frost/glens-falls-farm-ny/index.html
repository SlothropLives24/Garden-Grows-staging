Frost dates for Glens Falls Farm, New York

Half of years have their last spring frost by May 17, but 1 year in 10 still frosts after May 31.

A frost date is really a percentile from 30 years of records at a nearby weather station

in about half of years a frost still comes after the median date. Both numbers are below, so you can choose the risk yourself.

From NOAA’s own record at Glens Falls Farm, 1991–2020 · Well established

NOAA NCEI 1991–2020 Climate Normals, the Glens Falls Farm station (listed by NOAA as Glens Falls Farm, USC00303284), at 43.32, -73.68. These are that station’s own records, unshifted. The further your garden is from it, the less exactly they describe your ground. The planner finds the nearest station to your garden and lowers its confidence with distance, so no number is presented as if it were measured in your garden.

Cold air also pools in low ground on the clear, still nights when frost happens, so a hollow can freeze while a slope a few hundred feet away does not. No table can know that about your garden. A few seasons of your own logged frosts can.
