From NOAA’s own record at Royal Oak 2 SSW, 1991–2020 · Well established

NOAA NCEI 1991–2020 Climate Normals, the Royal Oak 2 SSW station (listed by NOAA as Royal Oak 2 SSW HCN, USC00187806), at 38.72, -76.19. These are that station’s own records, unshifted. The further your garden is from it, the less exactly they describe your ground. The planner finds the nearest station to your garden and lowers its confidence with distance, so no number is presented as if it were measured in your garden.

Cold air also pools in low ground on the clear, still nights when frost happens, so a hollow can freeze while a slope a few hundred feet away does not. No table can know that about your garden. A few seasons of your own logged frosts can.
