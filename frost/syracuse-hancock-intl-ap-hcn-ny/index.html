From NOAA’s own record at Syracuse Hancock International Airport, 1991–2020 · Well established

NOAA NCEI 1991–2020 Climate Normals, the Syracuse Hancock International Airport station (listed by NOAA as Syracuse Hancock Intl Ap HCN, USW00014771), at 43.11, -76.10. These are that station’s own records, unshifted. The further your garden is from it, the less exactly they describe your ground. The planner finds the nearest station to your garden and lowers its confidence with distance, so no number is presented as if it were measured in your garden.

Cold air also pools in low ground on the clear, still nights when frost happens, so a hollow can freeze while a slope a few hundred feet away does not. No table can know that about your garden. A few seasons of your own logged frosts can.
