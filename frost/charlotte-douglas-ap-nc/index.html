Half of years have their last spring frost by March 30, but 1 year in 10 still frosts after April 14.

From NOAA’s own record at Charlotte Douglas Airport, 1991–2020 · Well established

NOAA NCEI 1991–2020 Climate Normals, the Charlotte Douglas Airport station (listed by NOAA as Charlotte Douglas Ap, USW00013881), at 35.22, -80.96. These are that station’s own records, unshifted. The further your garden is from it, the less exactly they describe your ground. The planner finds the nearest station to your garden and lowers its confidence with distance, so no number is presented as if it were measured in your garden.

Cold air also pools in low ground on the clear, still nights when frost happens, so a hollow can freeze while a slope a few hundred feet away does not. No table can know that about your garden. A few seasons of your own logged frosts can.
