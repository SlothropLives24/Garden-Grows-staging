Frost dates in China

Frost dates for 111 cities in China, worked out from a weather model’s daily lows and graded A good hunch. Where a city has frost dates, they are odds, with a safer date beside each. Where frost is rare or comes only in some winters, its page says so and lists what to sow when. Where a weather station with 20 or more years of daily records stands near a city, the frost dates are that station’s own instead, graded Promising, and the city’s page names it. Where a weather station stands near a city, whether frost is rare or comes in some or most winters is that station’s own count, and the city’s page names it.
