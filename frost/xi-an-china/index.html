Frost dates for Xi'an, China

Half of years have their last spring frost by March 13, but 1 year in 10 still frosts after March 31.

The Xian weather station, 5 km (3 miles) from Xi'an, recorded a frost in 17 of its 17 winters on record from 1991 to 2020.

A frost date is really a percentile, here from 30 years (1991 to 2020) of a weather model’s daily lows near Xi'an

From a weather model, not a station, checked against Xian’s record · A good hunch

A station’s frost count with the model’s dates, graded A good hunch. Whether frost comes here, and how often, is the Xian weather station’s own count, 5 km (3 miles) away. Its dates come from the weather model’s square centered 14 km (8 miles) away.

If you know your own frost dates, enter them under your place on the Plan, and your plans go by yours, with these shown beside them. See where every city’s dates come from on the coverage map.

Modeled, not measured. No weather station stands behind these dates. They come from ERA5, a reconstruction of the world’s weather from 1991 to 2020 made by ECMWF, Europe’s medium-range weather forecasting agency, for the EU’s Copernicus program, and read here through Open-Meteo. They are the model’s daily lows for the point at 34.25, 108.75, 13 km (8 miles) from the center of Xi'an. A frost here means a low at or below 0 °C (32 °F).

A weather model sees the land in squares about 25 km (16 miles) across, so it can miss what sets one garden apart from the next: a frost hollow, a hillside, a lakeshore, a city’s own warmth. The Milpa planner reads these same numbers for a garden here and grades them A good hunch.

The frost count is a weather station’s. It comes from NOAA’s Global Historical Climatology Network daily records (GHCN-Daily) for the Xian station (CHM00057036), 5 km (3 miles) from Xi'an. A winter counts when the station recorded its three coldest months, and it is a frost winter when a night in it fell to 0 °C (32 °F) or below. Any frost dates on this page are the weather model’s.

Cold air also pools in low ground on the clear, still nights when frost happens, so a hollow can freeze while a slope a hundred meters away does not. No table can know that about your garden. A few seasons of your own logged frosts can.

The highs and lows here are the 1991 to 2020 normals of WMO weather station 0-20000-0-57131, 18 km (11 miles) from Xi'an at 411 m (1,348 ft) above sea level, not the weather model’s.
