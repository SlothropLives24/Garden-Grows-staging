Frost dates for Hamburg, Germany

Half of years have their last spring frost by April 24, but 1 year in 10 still frosts after May 9.

These frost dates are the Hamburg Fuhlsbuettel weather station’s own, 9 km (6 miles) from Hamburg, from 30 years of its daily lows in NOAA’s GHCN-Daily records. It recorded a frost in 29 of its 29 winters on record from 1991 to 2020. Hollows and low ground near your garden can frost later in spring and earlier in fall than the station does.

The weather model’s dates for Hamburg: last frost around April 12.

From the Hamburg Fuhlsbuettel weather station, 9 km (6 miles) away, 30 winters · Promising

A weather station’s own frost dates, graded Promising. Its dates come from the Hamburg Fuhlsbuettel weather station, 9 km (6 miles) away, with 30 complete years of daily lows.

If you know your own frost dates, enter them under your place on the Plan, and your plans go by yours, with these shown beside them. See where every city’s dates come from on the coverage map.

The frost dates are a weather station’s. They come from NOAA’s GHCN-Daily daily records for the Hamburg Fuhlsbuettel station (GM000010147), 9 km (6 miles) from Hamburg: Milpa read its daily lows for 1991 to 2020 and, in each of the 30 years it recorded in full, the last spring night and the first fall night at or below 0 °C (32 °F).

A weather station measures one spot, so the ground around it can differ: a frost hollow, a hillside, a lakeshore, a city’s own warmth. The Milpa planner reads these same dates for a garden here and grades them Promising.

The frost count is a weather station’s. It comes from NOAA’s Global Historical Climatology Network daily records (GHCN-Daily) for the Hamburg Fuhlsbuettel station (GM000010147), 9 km (6 miles) from Hamburg. A winter counts when the station recorded its three coldest months, and it is a frost winter when a night in it fell to 0 °C (32 °F) or below.

The weather model, for comparison. Its frost dates for Hamburg come from ERA5, a reconstruction of the world’s weather from 1991 to 2020 made by ECMWF, Europe’s medium-range weather forecasting agency, for the EU’s Copernicus program, and read here through Open-Meteo: its daily lows for the point at 53.75, 9.75, 27 km (17 miles) from the center of Hamburg.

Cold air also pools in low ground on the clear, still nights when frost happens, so a hollow can freeze while a slope a hundred meters away does not. No table can know that about your garden. A few seasons of your own logged frosts can.

The highs and lows here are the 1991 to 2020 normals of WMO weather station 00010147, 9 km (6 miles) from Hamburg at 11 m (36 ft) above sea level, not the weather model’s.
