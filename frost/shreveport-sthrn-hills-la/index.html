Frost dates for Shreveport Sthrn Hills, Louisiana

Half of years have their last spring frost by March 12, but 1 year in 10 still frosts after April 1.

A frost date is really a percentile from 30 years of records at a nearby weather station

in about half of years a frost still comes after the median date. Both numbers are below, so you can choose the risk yourself.

From NOAA’s own record at Shreveport Sthrn Hills, 1991–2020 · Well established

NOAA NCEI 1991–2020 Climate Normals, the Shreveport Sthrn Hills station (listed by NOAA as Shreveport Sthrn Hills, USC00168444), at 32.39, -93.78. These are that station’s own records, unshifted. The further your garden is from it, the less exactly they describe your ground. The planner finds the nearest station to your garden and lowers its confidence with distance, so no number is presented as if it were measured in your garden.

Cold air also pools in low ground on the clear, still nights when frost happens, so a hollow can freeze while a slope a few hundred feet away does not. No table can know that about your garden. A few seasons of your own logged frosts can.

Summer and rain
