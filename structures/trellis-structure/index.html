Cattle Panel

A support structure - any of: Cattle Panel, Bamboo Tripod, String Trellis. Not a plant; it earns its place by what it carries.

Carries
a climbing vine - effectively any load a garden asks
Cost
~$10–$40
Lasts
5–20 years, reused season after season

Fills the support role with zero nutrient competition, zero timing constraint, and effectively unbounded load. Loses biomass, ground cover, and the story.

Plantings that use it

  • Grape Arbour
  • Melon Sisters (Small Bed)
  • Sweet-Potato Milpa (Small Bed)
  • Three Sisters (Small Bed)

Where it goes: along the shaded (poleward) edge of the bed, so its shadow falls out of the growing ground - set with the planting, before the vine needs it.
